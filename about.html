A day with LuminAAC

Iwantjuice
Iwantjuicemorepleasestop

7:30 · Breakfast

A word lands where they can see it

Your child taps juice. The word travels from its tile into the sentence bar, so they learn where their words go. The board itself never moves: same place, same colour, every day.

11:00 · School

The Bridge keeps everyone on the same page

Their teacher sees new words and what a sound or a gesture means. When their tablet syncs, the change reaches every grown-up's phone, with end-to-end encryption if you turn it on.

Iwantgopark

16:00 · Play

Games made of their own words

Thirteen word games use the same tiles as their board. In Lantern Sky, each word floats up as a lantern and lines up into a sentence when they press Speak. Nothing fails, and "All done" always works.

19:30 · Calm

One slow rhythm at bedtime

The Calm sky rises as they breathe in for four seconds and settles as they breathe out for six. A grown-up chooses how much moves, and the tablet's "reduce motion" setting always wins.
