Why LuminAAC exists

Built by a dad who refused to accept it.

I'm a single dad. My son is nonverbal. Not yet verbal, I like to say. When we went looking for a voice for him, we found two kinds: ones that boxed him into someone else's idea of what he should say, and ones that locked his words behind a subscription.

I couldn't accept either. So I built the app I wish we'd been handed: one that learns how he already communicates, keeps his words on our own devices, and never asks for a credit card.

“No one should have to pay for their voice to be heard.”

The founder of LuminAAC, and a dad

See it speak

Five places, one voice.

These are real recordings of LuminAAC on a tablet. Nothing is staged.

  1. Tap, and it speaks.

    Colour-coded words that stay in the same place, so hands learn where they live. Build a sentence and say it out loud.

  2. A place to breathe.

    Show how big a feeling is, ask for what helps, and breathe with a slow light.

  3. Know what comes next.

    Visual plans for the store, the doctor and the morning. Tick off each step and say what you need along the way.

  4. Learning that feels like play.

    Stories and twenty games. In Lantern Sky, every word floats up as a lantern.

  5. For everyone who listens.

    Caregivers catch the moments that matter, learn which sounds mean what, and share it with the whole circle.
