Why LuminAAC exists

Built by a dad who refused to accept it.

I'm a single dad. My son is nonverbal. Not yet verbal, I like to say. When we went looking for a voice for him, we found two kinds: ones that boxed him into someone else's idea of what he should say, and ones that locked his words behind a subscription.

I couldn't accept either. So I built the app I wish we'd been handed: one that learns how he already communicates, keeps his words on our own devices, and never asks for a credit card.

“No one should have to pay for their voice to be heard.”

The founder of LuminAAC, and a dad

See it speak

Five places, one voice.

These are real recordings of LuminAAC on a tablet. Nothing is staged.

  1. Tap, and it speaks.

    Colour-coded words that stay in the same place, so hands learn where they live. Build a sentence and say it out loud.

  2. A place to breathe.

    Show how big a feeling is, ask for what helps, and breathe with a slow light.

  3. Know what comes next.

    Visual plans for the store, the doctor and the morning. Tick off each step and say what you need along the way.

  4. Learning that feels like play.

    Stories and twenty games. In Lantern Sky, every word floats up as a lantern.

  5. For everyone who listens.

    Caregivers catch the moments that matter, learn which sounds mean what, and share it with the whole circle.

New in November 2026

More ways to be heard.

Built with speech therapists' tools in mind, and still free. All of it can run on the device. The sentence helper is optional and off until a grown-up turns it on.

  • For the people who listen

    Show how to say it.

    Press their buttons as you talk, so they see where words live. Your presses leave a numbered trail and are kept apart from theirs.

  • For gestalt language learners

    Whole phrases, their way.

    Add the phrases they already love, from shows, songs or people. Say them whole, or split them into parts to mix with other words.

  • In Calm

    Something hurts.

    Press where, then how it feels, and LuminAAC says it: "My tummy feels sharp." Then ask for what would help.

  • In My day

    Choices and routines.

    Two to eight big pictures to choose from, made in the Bridge. Routines you write show up with Now and Then.

  • Optional

    Say it in a full sentence.

    Turn it on and ✨ offers fuller sentences, checked to keep their exact words and meaning. Their own words always come first.

  • For therapists and families

    A report you can check.

    What messages were for, sentence length and how much they said on their own. Every figure shows its source, and any reading can be corrected.

  • Find a word. Type it and LuminAAC opens the folder it lives in, so next time they know where to look. Word endings turn "eat" into "eating" or "ate".
  • Steadier hands and switches. Optional hold-to-choose and ignore-repeat-taps settings, and switch scanning that reaches every button.
  • Easy to read in every theme. Day, night and high contrast pass automated WCAG AA colour contrast checks.
