# LuminAAC brand voice guidelines

Version 1.0 · November 2026 · Owner: the founder of LuminAAC (ODASI Technologies Inc.)

These guidelines are the single source of truth for how LuminAAC sounds and looks in words: the website (luminaac.org), the app (luminaac.app), guides, store listings, social posts, emails, decks and grant applications. The discovery that produced them is in [docs/BRAND-DISCOVERY.md](../docs/BRAND-DISCOVERY.md).

---

## 1. What LuminAAC is, in one breath

LuminAAC is a free AAC app for nonverbal people. It learns a person's own language from the people who love them, and turns it into words everyone can understand. It is free for families, forever.

## 2. Taglines (decided)

| Role | Line | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead** | **No one should have to pay to be heard.** | Hero headings, store listing, social bios, the first line of any pitch |
| **Second** | **Finally, technology that sees what you see.** | Wherever we explain *how* it works: learning from caregivers, signals, moments |
| Supporting | Their voice. Not someone else's. | Dignity, personalisation, against "boxed" vocabularies |
| Supporting | A voice is a right, not a subscription. | Pricing, the manifesto, press |
| Supporting | You are their best translator. | Caregiver-facing copy, onboarding, the Bridge |

Never stack more than two taglines in one view.

## 3. Non-negotiable rules

1. **Free for families, forever.** There is no paid family tier, now or later. No trials, no "starter" plans, no ads. Schools and clinics that want professional tools help keep it free for everyone else. Never write "free core", "freemium" or "free to start".
2. **Privacy of the founder's family.** The founder's story is told in general terms only: "a single dad", "my son". Never his name, photos, age, school, town, diagnosis details or anything else that could identify him. No family photos anywhere public. Demo people in screenshots use invented names and say so.
3. **"Nonverbal people", not "nonverbal children"**, when describing who LuminAAC is for. Name children only when the context is specifically about children (a school, a pediatric clinic, a parent talking about their own child).
4. **Honest about limits.** Safety features help; they don't guarantee. Clinical features support judgement; they don't replace it. No HIPAA, FERPA, COPPA or FDA claims unless certified. No claimed outcomes ("improves speech by…") without published evidence.
5. **Sensing is optional.** Camera, microphone, wearables and sensors are optional features that only switch on when a caregiver turns them on. Always say so when you mention them. Never imply the app watches or records by default.
6. **No invented testimonials.** Quotes from families or clinicians must be real and used with permission. Illustrative stories are labelled as examples.

## 4. Voice

LuminAAC sounds like a calm, warm parent who also happens to be precise. Four attributes:

| Attribute | We are | We are not | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Warm** | personal, first-person when it's the founder, "you" to caregivers | gushing, sentimental, pitying | "I'm a single dad. My son is nonverbal. Not yet verbal, I like to say." |
| **Plain** | short sentences, everyday words, one idea per sentence | technical, clinical jargon, acronyms without explanation | "Tap the light. It speaks." |
| **Firm** | clear about money and dignity | aggressive toward competitors by name | "That isn't a launch offer. It's the point." |
| **Honest** | specific about what works today and what doesn't | hype, "revolutionary", "world's first", "AI magic" | "Draft. Review required." |

Tone shifts by audience, voice stays the same:

- **Families:** warmest. Reassuring, practical, never urgent. Lead with what they already know about their person.
- **Clinicians and schools:** still plain, more precise. Name the evidence and its limits. Keep "draft, review required" language.
- **Investors and partners:** confident and concrete. Vision is allowed, but label it as vision (see §7).
- **The person using My Voice:** the fewest words. Literal, kind, no idioms or sarcasm.

## 5. Words

**Use:** nonverbal people · not yet verbal · minimally speaking · AAC (spell out once: augmentative and alternative communication) · caregivers, families, care circle · signals, moments · My Voice, the Bridge · their voice · free for families, forever · on your own devices · optional · suggests (not "decides").

**Avoid:**

| Avoid | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| voiceless, "a voice to the voiceless" | they already communicate; it erases them | "people who can't speak yet", "their voice" |
| suffers from, afflicted, low-functioning | deficit language | describe needs, not labels |
| free core, freemium, premium (for families) | breaks the core promise | free for families, forever |
| revolutionary, groundbreaking, world's first, magic | hype; unverifiable | say what it does |
| polyvagal, Kp storms, 432 Hz, neuromorphic, quantum | unproven or irrelevant to families | leave out, or move to a clearly labelled research note |
| the AI knows / understands / reads emotions | overclaims | "estimates", "suggests", "asks you to confirm" |
| guarantees, prevents, keeps them safe | safety overclaims | "helps", "alerts you", "never replaces supervision" |
| patients, cases (in family copy) | clinical distance | people, your child, the person you support |

## 6. Messaging pillars

1. **Free, forever.** A voice is a right, not a subscription.
2. **Learns from you.** You are their best translator; LuminAAC turns what you know into words (the 15-second moment, "What did that mean?").
3. **Two sides, one voice.** My Voice for the person, the Bridge for everyone who supports them.
4. **Private by default.** Words and recordings stay on your own devices unless you choose to sync. Sensing is optional.
5. **Clinically grounded, honestly.** Built on AAC practice (core words that stay put, modelling, gestalt language support), with every limit stated.
6. **Help, not guarantees.** Safety alerts and water zones help a watching adult; they never replace one.

## 7. The Empathic Guardian vision

The long-range vision (ambient LuminNode cameras, AR point-and-speak, voice training, neuromorphic hardware) is used in two forms:

- **Investor and partner version:** the full whitepaper, clearly marked "Vision: not in the app today" on its cover and on every page that describes unbuilt features. Replace "voice to the voiceless" with "their voice". Remove unsupported regulatory claims, and the line about licensing data, unless it is rewritten as opt-in, de-identified and ethics-reviewed research.
- **Public version:** [docs/VISION.md](../docs/VISION.md), in plain language, which separates *what the app does today* from *what we hope to build*, and states that every sensing feature will be optional and off by default.

## 8. Story rules (the founder)

- Allowed: single dad; a son who is nonverbal ("not yet verbal"); searching for an AAC app and finding boxed vocabularies or subscriptions; understanding him better than any app could; that understanding staying behind when he was with others; building the app he wished they'd been handed; the promise to keep it free; building it with love.
- Not allowed: his name, image, age, school, location, medical details, specific identifiable events, family photos.
- Sign-off: "The founder of LuminAAC, and a dad."

## 9. Visual identity, in words

- **Logo:** a sky-blue wave under a gold sun. Wordmark "LuminAAC" (capital L, AAC in capitals).
- **Website:** "Daylight prism": pearl-white light, glass, a rainbow fringe where light bends, dark ink for every word. See [DESIGN-SITE.md](../DESIGN-SITE.md).
- **App:** calm and dark-or-light by preference, a lit gold that marks only the chosen thing, standard AAC word-class colours. See [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md).
- **Type:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading everywhere; Plus Jakarta Sans and Instrument Serif italic for website display only.
- **Retired:** the gold/teal/coral palette and paid fonts in the old Drive page "Technology That Sees What You See", and the "Night to light" website look. Don't use them in new material.

## 10. Checklist before anything goes out

- [ ] Does it say or imply families might pay? Remove it.
- [ ] Any name, photo or identifying detail of the founder's son? Remove it.
- [ ] "Nonverbal people" (unless children are the specific context)?
- [ ] Sensing mentioned? It says optional.
- [ ] Any claim of guarantees, certification or outcomes? Back it with evidence or remove it.
- [ ] Jargon a parent wouldn't use? Replace it.
- [ ] Max two taglines, and the lead one first?
- [ ] Testimonials real and permitted, or labelled as examples?
