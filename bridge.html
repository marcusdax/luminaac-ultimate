                            Water danger zones

                            Off

                            Many children who wander are drawn to water. LuminAAC can find mapped ponds, pools, rivers and lakes near your safe zones and send an urgent alert if your child gets close.

                            Turn on safe zones first.

                            30 m

                            Phone locations can be 10 to 30 m out. A bigger distance gives earlier warnings and more false alarms.

                            1.5 km

                              Water comes from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors) and is saved on your devices, so checks work offline. Maps can miss garden pools and new ponds: draw those yourself as a danger area. This adds to supervision; it never replaces it.

                              Location privacy

                              • Location is checked only on the My Voice device, only while it's open, and only after you turn this on.
                              • The device compares its position with your zones itself. It sends just "arrived" or "left", plus where, to your own LuminAAC server.
                              • Exact locations on alerts are deleted after 24 hours. No location history is kept.
                              • Teachers, therapists and share links never see location.

                              Recent zone events

                                When the app is closed

                                Honestly: a web app can't watch zones in the background. Browsers stop location as soon as My Voice is closed, the screen locks, or the tab is hidden. Installing it as an app helps it open faster and work offline, but it doesn't change this.

                                • Quiet-device alert: while My Voice shares location, it checks in every few minutes. If it goes quiet for longer than the time you choose, your server sends you an alert, so silence never looks like "all fine".
                                • Background zones: the LuminAAC phone app (built from the same code with Capacitor) can use the phone's own geofencing. See native/README.md.

                                    Sync, alerts and share links

                                    Optional. Connect to a LuminAAC server you run (see backend/) to sync between devices, get "I need help" alerts on your phone, and send read-only links to a teacher or therapist. Recordings never leave this device.

                                      Feeling estimates

                                      When you explain a recorded moment, LuminAAC can guess how it sounded (excited, calm, upset…) from pitch, loudness and pace. The guess is made on this device, is labelled as an estimate, and only counts once you confirm it.

                                      The speech feelings model isn't downloaded.

                                      Help improve suggestions for everyone

                                      Optional and off by default. LuminAAC can learn which suggestions tend to help (by time of day, place and feeling), right here on this device. If you join, only a small set of numbers is sent: it is clipped, blurred with random noise, and mixed with other families' numbers so the server can only see the total. Moments, words, recordings and names are never sent.

                                      Your data

                                      Everything is stored in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded.

                                        What did that mean?

                                        Turn on safe zones?

                                        Here's exactly what happens:

                                        • My Voice asks this device for its location while it's open. The browser will ask too, and you can say no there.
                                        • It checks the zones you draw on the device itself.
                                        • When your child arrives at or leaves a zone, you get an alert with where they are. "Take me home" includes where they are too.
                                        • Alerts go only to your family's LuminAAC server. Exact locations are deleted after 24 hours.
                                        • You can turn this off at any time. Turning it off deletes your zones.

                                        You know your child better than anyone.

                                        LuminAAC learns from you. Let's start with a few small things.

                                        Here's the whole idea:

                                        1. Catch it. Tap once to keep the last 15 seconds.
                                        2. Say what it meant. In your own words.
                                        3. Watch them be heard. My Voice starts offering the right words.

                                        Moments stay on this device unless you choose to share.
