# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want LuminAAC to be a welcoming community for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. This includes people who communicate with AAC.

## Our standards

Things that help:

- being kind, patient and respectful, including of different ways of communicating
- presuming competence: every person has something to say
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- putting the wellbeing and privacy of children and families first

Things we don't accept:

- harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, including about disability
- sharing anyone's private information, especially a child's, without explicit permission
- sexualised language or imagery
- trolling or personal or political attacks
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public. Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainer through GitHub. Every report will be reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors, who break this code.
