# Where LuminAAC is going

*A plain-language version of our long-range vision, sometimes called "The Empathic Guardian". It separates what the app does today from what we hope to build. Nothing in the second half is in the app yet.*

## Why

Every nonverbal person is already communicating: a sound, a look, a change in breathing, a hand that reaches. The people who love them learn that language over time, but the knowledge lives in their heads. When that person is with a teacher, a sitter or a new therapist, it stays behind.

LuminAAC exists to carry that understanding everywhere, and to do it free for families, forever. No one should have to pay to be heard.

## What LuminAAC does today

- **My Voice**, a talker with core words that never move, natural voices, quick phrases, scripts for people who learn language in whole phrases, a body map for "where it hurts", and choices.
- **The Bridge**, for families and caregivers: catch a 15-second moment, say what it meant, and watch My Voice start offering the right words. Signal library, stories, sharing with the care circle, team chat, and a progress report for therapists, marked "draft, review required".
- **Optional help**, all off until a caregiver turns it on: safe zones and water danger zones, wearable trackers and sensors, Potty Path, Sound Path, meaning search and a sentence helper.
- **Private by default.** Words and recordings stay on your own devices unless you choose to sync. It works offline.

Safety features help a watching adult. They never replace one.

## What we hope to build

These are ideas, not promises. Each would be **optional, off by default, and switched on only by a caregiver**, with recordings kept on the family's own devices.

1. **Point and speak.** Point the camera at something, an apple or a swing, and My Voice offers the words for it.
2. **A shared day timeline.** Moments, words and notes on one timeline, so a caregiver can say "that's what she meant" after the fact and the app learns from it.
3. **Room sensors (LuminNode).** Small optional room cameras or sensors that help catch signals a tablet misses. Only for families who want them, and never required.
4. **Practising sounds and words.** Gentle, game-like practice for people who want to try speaking, without making communication depend on speech.
5. **Better suggestions.** Learning which words a person reaches for at which times and places, and offering them sooner. Suggestions are always suggestions.

## What we won't do

- Charge families. Not now, not later.
- Turn on a camera, microphone or sensor without a caregiver choosing to.
- Sell personal data. Any research use would be opt-in, de-identified and reviewed by an ethics board.
- Claim medical outcomes, certifications or guarantees we can't show.
