# LuminAAC Ultimate — Complete User Guide for Families, Schools & Clinics

*Step-by-Step Handbook for My Voice (Child Communicator) and The Bridge (Caregiver & SLP Portal)*

## 1. Quick Start & Everyday Setup

- **Open Anywhere (Zero Installation Required):** Open index.html for My Voice (the child's communication board) and bridge.html for The Bridge (the caregiver, teacher, and SLP portal). Both can be installed as offline apps (PWAs) from your browser menu.
- **Everything Saves Automatically on Your Device:** Your child's custom buttons, recorded voice clips, learned signals, schedules, and biometric logs are stored locally on your device. No account is required.
- **Caregiver Lock (Hold for 1.2 Seconds):** In My Voice, the Caregiver tab is protected by a 1.2-second press-and-hold so a child Exploring the board or using switch scanning never accidentally lands in settings.

## 2. Using My Voice — Core Board, Modeling, Gestalts & Quick-Bar

- **Core Words Stay in One Place:** The 30 Core words (I, want, more, help, stop, go, eat, drink, etc.) never move when folders or time-of-day rules change. Tap words to build a sentence in the top strip, or tap 'Speak' to say the whole strip aloud.
- **Sensory Quick-Bar (Bottom of Screen):** Five essential self-advocacy buttons — Stop, No, Help, Bathroom, and Break — are always reachable at the bottom of the Talk screen for immediate 1-tap expression.
- **Partner Modeling Mode (Aided Language Stimulation):** Tap 'Model for child' (#modelToggle) below the grid when you want to point out words while talking with your child. Tapped tiles glow with a 5-word visual trail and will NEVER pollute your child's own word counts or MLU stats.
- **Gestalt Scripts & Stage 2 Mitigation:** Under 'Gestalts & scripts' (or More -> My phrases), tap any whole phrase to speak it (or play your 5-second recorded voice). For Stage 2+ phrases, tap 'Mitigate into strip' to split the script into movable chunks in the sentence bar.
- **Vocabulary Search & Word Endings (Morphology):** Type in 'Find a word' (#vsearch) to search by prefix, stem, or meaning (e.g., typing 'thirsty' finds 'drink' and 'water'). When a word is in the sentence strip, the 'Change to' bar (#morphBar) offers one-tap grammar endings (-s, -ing, -ed, -er).

## 3. Sound Symphony Composer, Lip-Viseme Coach & Acoustic Co-Regulation

- **Sound Symphony & Lip Coach (More -> Sound symphony):** Pick a target word (like 'more', 'water', 'mama', 'dada', 'help', 'bubble') or tap any of the 16 musical sound blocks (mmm, buh, puh, wuh, ah, oh, eh, ee, etc.). Each tap plays a warm pentatonic note and animates the SVG mouth/lip posture showing how lips and tongue move.
- **Blend Sounds into a Spoken Word:** After tapping 2 to 4 sound blocks (for example, m + oh + r), tap 'Play Symphony & Speak' to hear the melody resolve into the spoken word!
- **Acoustic Co-Regulation Soundscapes (Calm View & More -> My body):** Tap any of the 5 gentle Web Audio soundscapes to support sensory regulation: Warm Pink Noise Shield (masks sudden classroom/household noises), Deep Brown Noise Blanket, 432 Hz Warm Harmonic Drone, 528 Hz Gentle Chime Pad, or 6 Hz Theta Co-Regulation Pulse. Tap 'Stop soundscape' anytime.

## 4. Body & Pain Check-In, Choice Boards & First-Then Schedules

- **Interoception Body & Pain Check-In (Calm View & More -> My body):** Your child can tap a body part (Head, Ears, Tummy, Teeth, Throat, Chest, Back, Arms, Legs, Skin, or Private area) and how it feels (Hurts, Sharp, Itchy, Hot, Cold, Tight, Sick). My Voice speaks the exact sentence ('My tummy hurts') and offers immediate relief tiles ('Bathroom', 'Water', 'Take a break', 'I need help').
- **Choice Boards & First-Then Schedules (Caregiver -> Phrases, choices and boards):** Create 2-to-8 item Choice Boards (e.g., 'Snack time: apple, banana, crackers') and multi-step First-Then Visual Schedules (e.g., 'Bath time: Take off clothes -> Bath -> Pajamas') that appear automatically in Plans and Talk.

## 5. Holistic Diet, Gut-Brain & Solar Storm (Kp) Biometrics

- **Logging Meals, Textures, Hydration & GI Comfort:** In My Voice -> Caregiver (under 'Holistic Diet, Gut-Brain & Solar Geomagnetic (Kp) Biometrics'), log what your child ate, its sensory texture (Crunchy, Smooth, Chewy, Warm Liquid, Cold/Crisp), hydration in mL, and GI comfort (Comfortable, Mild Gas, Constipated, Reflux, Stomach Pain).
- **Checking Live NOAA Solar Kp & Sensory Correlations:** Tap 'Check live NOAA solar Kp' (or use the built-in offline Solar Cycle 25 estimator) to log geomagnetic storm activity (Kp 0–9) alongside your child's sensory state. LuminAAC automatically computes draft correlation insights showing how G1+ solar storms (Kp >= 5) or GI discomfort align with sensory overload or pain logs.

## 6. Expressive Child Voices, Motor Access & Switch Scanning

| Setting Area | How to Configure | Recommended Clinical Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kokoro-82M Child Voices & Pitch | My Voice -> Caregiver -> Voice, or Bridge -> Privacy -> On-device AI | Choose child-friendly presets (af_sky, af_nicole, am_puck, bf_isabella) and adjust pitch semitones [-12, +12] for a warm, personal voice. |
| Touch Hold (acceptMs) & Tremor Debounce (debounceMs) | Bridge -> Vocabulary -> Access settings | Increase Hold time (e.g., 150–300ms) to ignore accidental brushes; increase Tremor bounce lockout (e.g., 250–500ms) to prevent double-taps. |
| 1-Switch & 2-Switch Scanning | Bridge -> Vocabulary -> Access settings -> Switch scanning | Choose Linear, Row-Column (fast grid navigation), or 2-Switch Step Scan (Space/Enter). Enable Spoken scan cues for auditory-first learners. |
| Themes & Low-Arousal Mode | My Voice -> Caregiver -> Theme | Switch between Day, Night, High-Contrast (WCAG AAA >= 7:1), and Low-Arousal (zero motion, muted sensory colors). |

## 7. The Bridge — Magic Moments, BYOK Expansion, Clinical Exports & Sync

- **Catching a 15-Second Magic Moment (Bridge -> Today):** Tap 'Keep the last 15 seconds' whenever your child makes a meaningful sound, gesture, or look. Type what it meant in the Annotation Studio, confirm or reject the draft feeling estimate, and link it to a Signal.
- **Open Board Format (.obf / .obz) Import & Export:** In My Voice -> Caregiver, import existing .obf or .obz board files from CoughDrop, Cboard, or Snap, or click 'Download my boards (.obz)' to back up or share your boards.
- **Clinical Communication Matrix & IEP Exports:** Export formula-safe CSV and JSON clinical reports containing isolated child MLU, unique words, Core vs. Fringe ratios, Communication Matrix categories, and append-only revision history.
